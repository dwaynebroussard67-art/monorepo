# Build Order and Rationale

## First: Notion killer
Why:
- most defensible core invention
- enterprise pain is real
- a narrow MVP can demonstrate a big idea quickly

## Second: Calendly killer
Why:
- easiest to commercialize quickly
- straightforward integration surface
- immediate business ROI story

## Third: Shopify killer
Why:
- highest engineering surface area
- strongest demo impact after the first two are validated
